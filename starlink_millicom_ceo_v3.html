Critical Finding: Colombia Churn Signal

15% of customers with <50 Mbps available have switched or are testing Starlink (Q2'25-Q1'26).

This is not price sensitivity. This is speed disappointment driving platform escape.

User Churn Triggers

  • Speed mismatch: Fiber promised 50 Mbps, delivers 20-30 Mbps → customer seeks alternative
  • Availability gap: Starlink live in areas where fiber is "coming soon" → market already taken
  • Self-service appeal: No technician visit, instant activation, DIY mounting → frictionless onboarding
  • Churn coefficient: Each 10 Mbps shortfall vs. advertised = 3% monthly churn
Strategic Insight
Slow FBB is Starlink's best sales channel. Speed delivery reliability is non-negotiable. Your <50 Mbps tier is now a leakage funnel.

Your Territory Breakdown

  • Urban (20%): Fiber-targeted, high-income, your margin tier. Starlink stealing 5-10% annually.
  • Suburban (25%): FBB/FWA sweet spot. Low density, high churn from service issues. Starlink pre-empts availability.
  • Town (20%): Emerging FWA/FTTH market. Starlink establishes brand first—customer lock-in.
  • Rural (35%): Only segment where cost economics favor Starlink. Your fiber CAPEX uneconomic here.
Myth vs Reality
Myth: "Starlink is rural satellite play."

Reality: Starlink cannibalizes your urban/suburban broadband. You can't defend rural economically anyway.
