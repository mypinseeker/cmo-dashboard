## SLIDE 5: Pillar A — MVP (Mobile Consumer)

### Opportunity
- Colombia MNO market: **$6.97B (2026) → $7.89B (2031)**
- 5G subscribers expected to reach **8M by 2030**
- Tigo+Movistar combined: 30M+ subscribers — **scale for 5G ROI**

### Huawei Solution

| Solution | Benefit | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **SingleRAN** | One radio platform for 2G/3G/4G/5G | **30% CAPEX savings** on network consolidation |
| **CloudRAN** | Centralized baseband, distributed radios | **25% OPEX reduction** (energy + maintenance) |
| **5G NSA→SA Migration** | Phased 5G deployment, protect 4G investment | De-risk 5G rollout |
| **AI-powered Network Optimization** | Auto parameter tuning, predictive maintenance | **15% capacity gain** without new sites |
| **Massive MIMO** | 64T64R for urban capacity boost | **3-5x capacity** in congested areas |

### Network Integration Fast Track
> Post-merger, Tigo has TWO radio networks. Huawei's SingleRAN can consolidate them onto **one platform** in 18 months — vs 36+ months with multi-vendor approach.

### Reference
- Huawei + Claro Brazil: 5G deployment in São Paulo, 40% faster rollout vs original timeline
- Huawei + América Móvil: 23 countries, proven LATAM track record

## SLIDE 6: Pillar B — FBB (Fixed Broadband)

### Opportunity
- Colombia fixed broadband penetration: **only 46%** (2024) — massive room to grow
- FTTH gaining share: expected to **surpass cable by 2028**
- Secondary cities (Barranquilla, Bucaramanga): fiber penetration **<40%** — blue ocean
- Claro already launched **5G FWA home internet** (June 2024, 160GB plans)

### Huawei Solution

| Solution | Benefit | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **GPON / XGS-PON** | Cost-effective FTTH for mass deployment | **50% lower cost per home** vs traditional |
| **FWA (Fixed Wireless Access)** | Rapid rural coverage without digging | **3x faster deployment** than fiber in rural |
| **WiFi 7 Home Gateway** | Premium home experience (gaming, streaming) | **Higher ARPU** from premium plans |
| **Smart ONT** | Remote diagnostics, self-service | **40% reduction** in truck rolls |
| **Convergent Platform** | Mobile + Home bundle management | **25% lower churn** from bundled customers |

### Strategy: Dual-Track Deployment
```
Urban Centers (Bogotá, Medellín, Cali):
  → GPON/XGS-PON FTTH — fiber to every building

Secondary Cities (Barranquilla, Bucaramanga, Pereira):
  → FTTH where feasible + FWA for rapid coverage

Rural Areas:
  → 5G FWA exclusively — meet 85% government target
```

### Reference
- Tigo Honduras: **$1B network investment** announced, including FTTH expansion
- Millicom LATAM: 14M homes passed with cable — Huawei can upgrade to GPON
