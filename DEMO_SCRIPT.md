# CMO 大屏客户演示脚本

> **客户**: Tigo Colombia
> **受众**: CMO 及管理层
> **时长**: 30-45 分钟
> **语言**: 英文为主，可切换中文

---

## 演示流程（6 步）

### Step 1: 开场 — "10 秒看全局"（3 分钟）

**动作**: 打开大屏首页，停在总览界面

**讲述要点**:
> "This dashboard gives your CMO a single view of the entire network — users, experience, and business — updated daily."

指向顶部三个 KPI：
- **12.5M** active users — growing +2.3% MoM
- **Experience score** — and how it compares to Claro
- **Alert cities** — where attention is needed right now

**关键一句话**:
> "Your CMO opens this every morning and knows in 10 seconds: are we growing, is experience improving, and where do we need to act."

---

### Step 2: 用户维度 — "谁在增长、谁在流失"（5 分钟）

**动作**: 指向左上用户卡片

**讲述要点**:

1. 指向 52 周趋势图：
> "This 12-month trend shows steady user growth from 11.8M to 12.5M — but the growth rate is slowing."

2. 指向区域警报：
> "More importantly, we can immediately see WHERE the problems are:
> - Barranquilla is losing users: -1.2%
> - Cartagena is also declining: -0.8%
> - While Bogotá and Medellín are growing healthy."

3. **点击 Barranquilla** → 触发下钻：
> "Let's drill down into Barranquilla to understand WHY."

4. 在下钻页面指出诊断条（红色）：
> "The system automatically diagnoses: users declining + experience dropping + churn rising + Claro leading = HIGH PRIORITY."

5. 返回总览

---

### Step 3: 体验维度 — "跟自己比、跟对手比"（8 分钟）

**动作**: 指向右上体验卡片

**讲述要点**:

1. 演示下拉菜单切换：
> "The CMO can switch between different KPIs — download speed, upload speed, latency, coverage."

2. 选择"下行速率"，指向趋势图：
> "Our download speed has been improving — from 33 Mbps in December to 38.2 Mbps now."

3. 指向 vs Claro 对比：
> "But Claro is still ahead at 42.5 Mbps. The gap is 10% — however, it's CLOSING. That's the good news."

4. **点击地图上的 [网络指标] 按钮**：
> "The map now shows download speed by region — green means good, red means poor."

5. **切换到 [vs Claro] 按钮**：
> "Now we see the competitive gap — where are we ahead of Claro, where are we behind."
> "Medellín: we LEAD Claro by 5.6%. But Barranquilla: we're behind by nearly 50%."

6. 指向区域排名：
> "The ranking shows exactly where to invest: Barranquilla and Cartagena are the priority."

---

### Step 4: 覆盖分析 — "现网 vs Ookla 基准"（8 分钟）

**动作**: 点击地图 [覆盖分析] 按钮

**讲述要点**:

1. 指向 RSRP 分布图表（地图底部弹出）：
> "This is the coverage fingerprint of your network. The blue bars show how many users are connected at each signal level."

2. 指向三条数据：
> "Three layers of data in one chart:
> - Blue bars: YOUR actual users from MR data — where they actually do business
> - Cyan line: YOUR Ookla speedtest samples — how users experience your network
> - Orange dashed line: CLARO's Ookla samples — the competitive benchmark"

3. 指向摘要数字：
> "61.5% of your users have good coverage. But 38.5% — that's 4.82 MILLION people — are on weak signal. Claro's number? 70%. They have better coverage."

4. **切换 Tigo/Claro 热力图**：
> "We can also overlay coverage heatmaps — Tigo in blue, Claro in orange. You can see where Claro covers but Tigo doesn't."

---

### Step 5: 业务维度 — "用户在干什么、体验如何"（5 分钟）

**动作**: 指向底部 APP 卡片

**讲述要点**:

1. 指向 YouTube 饼图和数字：
> "YouTube: 32% of video sessions are below 480p quality. That's increasing — meaning video experience is getting WORSE."

2. 对比 Netflix：
> "Netflix is better: HD ratio at 43% and improving. The difference? Netflix has better adaptive streaming, but also: Netflix users tend to be on WiFi."

3. **点击 YouTube 卡片** → 弹出详情面板：
> "In the detail panel, we see the full resolution breakdown over 12 weeks — the stacked area chart shows how each quality tier changes over time."

4. 指向城市 HD 对比：
> "And by city: Barranquilla has only 18% HD — while Bogotá has 38%. This correlates directly with network congestion."

---

### Step 6: 终端 + 潜力 — "高价值用户在哪、增长机会在哪"（5 分钟）

**动作**: 指向左下终端卡片

**讲述要点**:

1. 指向阶梯图：
> "We track the device ecosystem — iPhone 16 Pro Max is the #1 device. These high-value device users represent 36% of subscribers but 68% of revenue."

2. **点击某个终端型号** → 展开大区分布：
> "Click any device and see its distribution across regions. This tells you WHERE your high-value customers are."

3. 指向预付费潜力标签：
> "1.2 million of these high-value device users are still on PREPAID. They're your best candidates for postpaid conversion."

4. **点击地图 [流失分布] 按钮**：
> "Now overlay churn data — see where you're losing users. Barranquilla has the highest churn at 2.1%. Cross-reference with experience data: poor network = high churn."

---

## 总结话术（3 分钟）

> "To summarize what this dashboard gives your CMO:

> 1. **Users**: Who's growing, who's churning, and where
> 2. **Experience**: How you compare to yourself over time, and how you compare to Claro
> 3. **Business**: What apps your users use, and whether video quality is improving or degrading
> 4. **Coverage**: Where your network is strong, where it's weak, and how it compares to competition — using both your own MR data and independent Ookla benchmarks
> 5. **Terminals**: Where your high-value customers are, and which ones are ready to convert

> All of this in ONE screen. No switching between 5 different tools. No waiting for reports. Real-time, every day."

## Q&A 准备（5-10 分钟）

### 预期问题及回答

| 客户可能问 | 回答要点 |
|:---|:---|
| 数据怎么接入？ | MR/话统通过现有 OMC 接口；Ookla 通过 Speedtest Intelligence API；探针通过 DPI 系统 |
| 多久更新一次？ | 网络 KPI 小时级；Ookla 月/季度级；可配置 |
| 能加更多城市吗？ | 架构支持任意数量的区域，只需配置数据源 |
| 安全性？ | 数据本地部署，不上公有云；可部署在客户自有基础设施 |
| 成本？ | 分阶段：Phase 1 数据接入 + Phase 2 定制化 + Phase 3 部署运维 |
| 跟华为其他产品的关系？ | 可与 SmartCare / MAE 集成，或独立部署 |

## 演示前检查清单

- [ ] 大屏分辨率设为 3840×2160（4K）
- [ ] 浏览器全屏（F11）
- [ ] 网络连接正常（GeoJSON 和 CDN 需要联网）
- [ ] 语言设为英文（点 EN 按钮）
- [ ] 地图默认显示全国视图
- [ ] 测试下钻功能可用
- [ ] 测试上滑面板可用
- [ ] 测试热力图切换可用
