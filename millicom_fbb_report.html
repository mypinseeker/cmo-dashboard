🛡️ Starlink Defense Strategy (Group-Wide)

Dimension Starlink Tigo FWA Tigo FTTH

Local Support None Physical stores Physical stores

Defense Tactics by Segment

Rural Markets

Deploy FWA at 1/3 Starlink price. Price advantage is unbeatable. ARPU $13-25 vs $50+.

Suburban Growth

FWA + Convergence bundles (mobile+home discounts). Starlink has no bundle strategy.

Urban Centers

FTTH speed/latency superiority is decisive. Starlink cannot compete on performance (500+ Mbps vs 50-200).

⚠️ Risk Management & Guardrails

Execution risks and trigger-based mitigation strategies:

Risk 1: Leverage Spike Trigger: Leverage >2.8x

Condition: Q2 or Q3 earnings confirm leverage exceeds 2.8x

Action: Pause P2 country investments immediately. Reallocate budget to P0 only.

Risk 2: FWA Underperformance Trigger: Adoption <50% forecast

Condition: After 6 months, FWA customer acquisition <50% of plan

Action: Scale back FWA, accelerate FTTH deployment instead

Risk 3: Claro Price War Trigger: Price cut >30%

Condition: Claro FBB pricing drops >30% in any market

Action: Pivot to convergence bundle differentiation (mobile+home), not price matching

Risk 4: Starlink Disruption Trigger: Monthly cost <$30

Condition: Starlink monthly subscription drops below $30

Action: Accelerate FTTH fiber deployment to urban/suburban. Cede rural if necessary.

Risk 5: Integration Delay (Colombia) Trigger: >12M without Phase 1 completion

Condition: Dual-network integration (Tigo+Coltel) not complete after 12 months

Action: Deploy Huawei integration SWAT team + external SI partner
