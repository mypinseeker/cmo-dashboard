"Deploy First, Build Later" Strategy

Dual-track FBB expansion combining wireless and fiber, validated by demand probes

Strategic Approach

Phase 1: WTTx Probe

Deploy fixed wireless broadband (FWA) in low-load 4G cells to test demand, geography, and willingness-to-pay

Phase 2: FWA Rapid

Scale FWA across validated zones; lock users with equipment subsidies and long-term contracts

Phase 3: FTTH Build

Fiber deployment only in high-ROI zones identified by FWA demand data (eliminate guesswork)

Key Advantage: FWA acts as a low-risk market validation tool, reducing capex waste. Fiber capex (USD 500–800/connection) only deployed where demand is proven.

Risk Analysis & Mitigation

Three primary risks with mitigation strategies

Risk 1: MNP Churn Exceeds Projections

Likelihood: Medium | Impact: High

If competitive pricing or service quality triggers 15%+ churn (vs. projected 5–10%), revenue impact ≈ USD 50M+ annually.

Mitigation: Pre-MNP Massive MIMO expansion prioritized in high-ARPU zones; real-time churn prediction enabling rapid sales intervention; retention budgets pre-approved.

Risk 2: FWA ROI Worse Than Modeled

Likelihood: Medium | Impact: High

FWA payback extends to 18+ months due to higher churn, lower ARPU, or network congestion forcing market withdrawal.

Mitigation: Rigorous pilot phase (Phase 1: 5K–10K units, 6-month ROI validation); strict network load threshold (≤70%) gates scaling; dynamic pricing model adjusts ARPU targets.

Risk 3: Huawei Solutions Differentiation Insufficient

Likelihood: Low | Impact: Medium

If Claro/Ericsson/Nokia achieve competitive feature parity, investment advantage erodes; premium pricing leverage weakens.

Mitigation: Exclusive local data science applications (coffee IoT models, Starlink defense playbook); deep Huawei partnership locks ecosystem; establish Guatemala innovation center co-branded with local universities.
