§4 Customer Distribution
60%+ of Starlink users are in urban, suburban, and town zones — not rural. This is direct market cannibalization, not incremental growth.

User Location Distribution

Key Insight

This is not a rural problem. This is a market-share problem.

Urban + Suburban + Town = 65% of Starlink users = direct overlap with your profitable service areas.

Competitive Implication

Myth: "Starlink only captures rural demand."

Reality: Starlink is taking customers in areas with FBB/MBB coverage—where your margin is highest.

Service Area Overlap

  • Urban (20%): High-income fiber-targeted customers
  • Suburban (25%): FBB/FWA sweet spot; low density but high churn from service issues
  • Town (20%): Emerging FWA/FTTH markets; Starlink pre-empts first-mover advantage
  • Rural (35%): Only here do cost economics favor Starlink (your fiber is uneconomic anyway)
Strategic conclusion: Abandon rural satellite defense. Defend suburban + town broadband markets where profitability exists.

§5 Strategic Framework
Three-tier deployment: FTTH in urban, FWA in suburban/town, skip rural. FWA ROI: 13-14 months.

3-Layer Defense Strategy

🏙️ Urban — Fiber (FTTH)

Weapon: FTTH delivering >500 Mbps, ultra-low latency, reliability.

Tactic: Speed + experience crushs satellite (300+ Mbps gaps). Capture high-value customers before Starlink V3.

ROI: 18-24 months | High margin

🏘️ Suburban/Town — FWA

Weapon: 4G/5G FWA deployed from existing towers. Fast deployment, low CAPEX.

Tactic: Block Starlink market entry by delivering 50-150 Mbps 6-12 months before Starlink penetrates. FWA is profitable.

ROI: 13-14 months | Profitable

🌾 Rural — Starlink

Decision: Don't compete. Cost-to-serve >> revenue per user. Let Starlink own this segment.

Exception: Govt subsidized USF projects only (if economics work with subsidy).

ROI: Negative | Strategic surrender

FWA Economics Proof

Pilot market deployment shows:

  • CAPEX: $150-200 per broadband pass
  • Revenue: $35-45 ARPU/month
  • Payback: 13-14 months
  • Margin post-payback: 60%+

Window Duration

Now → Q3 2026 (12-18 months): Your competitive advantage window.

After V3 deployment, Starlink achieves parity on speed + coverage. Your only margin is installation + service quality.
